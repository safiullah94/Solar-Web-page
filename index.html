Common
Questions

Clear answers to all your solar energy questions. Everything you need before making the switch.

01

Does it work in rain?

Yes, our high-efficiency cells capture ambient light even during heavy rain or clouds. The glass is specifically treated to ensure water runs off instantly.

02

What is the warranty?

All our systems come with a minimum 10-year product warranty and a 25-year performance guarantee, ensuring your investment is fully protected.

03

Can I add batteries later?

Absolutely. All SolarSux systems are designed to be battery-ready. Storage can be retrofitted at any point with zero disruption to your existing setup.

04

How long is the install?

Residential installs typically complete within 1–2 days. Commercial and industrial projects are scoped individually, with most completed within 2 weeks.
